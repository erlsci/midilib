# midilib audit — consumer fit (the API as `midi`/`midiio` will call it)

**Date:** 2026.06.17
**Project root:** `/Users/oubiwann/lab/erlsci/midilib`
**Dimension covered:** (4) fitness of midilib's *public surface* for its downstream consumers.
**Consumer evidence (read for this report):** `../midi/workbench/NEEDS-from-midiio.md` (requirements R1–R9), `../midi/workbench/SESSION-BOOTSTRAP-api-planning.md`, `../midi/workbench/UPSTREAM-minimidio.md`, `../midiio/workbench/SESSION-BOOTSTRAP-nif-planning.md`, `../midiio/workbench/RESPONSE-to-midi-needs.md`, `../midiio/workbench/UPSTREAM-minimidio-raw-api.md`. Requirement IDs (R1, R6, R7, …) refer to `NEEDS-from-midiio.md`.

## Executive summary

midilib is the codec the whole erlsci MIDI family routes through: `midiio` (codec-free byte transport) → **`midilib`** (`midibin:encode/1` outbound, `midibin:decode/1` on every inbound message, `midifile:read/1` streamed through the same send path) → `midi` (glue) → `undermidi`. Judged as the consumer will actually call it, the public surface has one confirmed Blocker — **multi-byte SysEx is uncodable** (R7), so every real inbound SysEx degrades to `{unknown, Bin}` — plus three High gaps: the **three-vocabulary split** that breaks the family's stated "one message representation across realtime and files," an **incompatible batch shape** between `midimsg:batch` and `midibin`'s batch codec, and an **untagged/forked error contract** that gives `midi` nothing uniform to branch on. The transport contract midilib depends on (one-complete-message-per-delivery, byte-exact, `midi` owns normalization) is otherwise *well matched* — `midibin:decode/1` is genuinely one-message-per-call and does not normalize vel-0 note-on, which is exactly what the consumer asked for. **There is no architecture/design document in midilib**, so the de-facto contract had to be reconstructed from the consumer docs above and from source; that gap is itself a consumer risk, since `midi` is being designed against undocumented midilib behaviour.

## Correctness / soundness (codec completeness)

### C1. Multi-byte SysEx cannot be encoded or decoded — inbound SysEx is dead for the family  — **Blocker** (R7)
**Location:** `src/midibin.erl:137-138` (decode), `:410-411` (encode); producer `src/midimsg.erl:304-306`
**What's wrong:** The codec handles exactly one 7-bit SysEx data byte. `midi`'s inbound path is `midiio → midibin:decode/1 → fan out`; a real device SysEx, delivered whole by `midiio` and re-serialised as `<<0xF0, …, 0xF7>>`, matches no `decode/1` clause and returns `{unknown, Bin}` (`:158-159`). On the outbound side, `midibin:encode/1` of `midimsg:sys_ex(Binary)` throws `badarg` (the `Data:7` segment requires an integer).
**Why it's wrong:** `NEEDS-from-midiio.md` R7 names this the family blocker: *"`midibin` only handles a single 7-bit data byte of SysEx … will hit `decode/1`'s catch-all and come back as `{unknown, Bin}`. This is a midilib fix … and it gates inbound SysEx for the whole family."* `midiio` already delivers SysEx whole (`UPSTREAM-minimidio-raw-api.md`: *"SysEx is delivered whole, 0xF0 … 0xF7, in a single callback"*), so the gap is entirely midilib's. Cross-ref erlang report #8, spec report S1–S2.
**Fix:** model the SysEx payload as a `binary()` end-to-end (decode `<<0xF0, Payload/binary>>` to the trailing `0xF7`; encode `<<0xF0, Bytes/binary, 0xF7>>`). Coordinate with R7's transport caveat (CoreMIDI virtual-source >~256 B fails — `UPSTREAM-minimidio.md` U1 — not midilib's bug, but the codec must not assume small payloads).

### C2. Three incompatible vocabularies break "one message representation across realtime and files"  — **High** (R6, R1)
**Location:** `src/midimsg.erl:11-16` & `README.md:31` (the unity claim); `src/midifile.erl` events `{Name, DeltaTime, Values}`; `src/midimsg.erl` `{midi, {Name, Data}}`; `src/midibin.erl` codec terms (a subset of the `midimsg` shapes).
**What's wrong:** The consumer's central design premise is `SESSION-BOOTSTRAP-api-planning.md`: *"One message representation across realtime and files. The same `{midi, ...}` terms flow through live device I/O and through `midifile:read/1`."* But the three modules speak three shapes. `midimsg:copyright/1` → `{midi, {copyright, Text}}`; `midifile` read → `{copyright, DeltaTime, Data}`; `midibin` → no clause for either (`ERR_MIDI_UNSUP`). Every `midimsg` meta constructor (copyright, lyric, marker, text, instrument, smpte, time_sig, keysig, sequence_number, …) is un-encodable by `midibin` and shaped differently from `midifile`'s events.
**Why it's wrong:** `midi` is promised one representation it can stream through both paths; instead it must translate between `midifile`'s 3-tuples and `midibin`'s `{midi, …}` terms, and the `midimsg` meta constructors connect to neither codec. The README/header claim of a common form is actively misleading to an integrator. Cross-ref erlang report #12 (root cause).
**Fix:** define one canonical term type (recommend `midifile` events adopt the `{midi, {Name, …}}` envelope, or `midibin`/`midifile` agree a shared `event()` type) and make `midibin:encode/1` accept every shape `midimsg` produces or explicitly reject it with a documented reason. Until then, `midi` cannot rely on the unity claim — say so in the README.

### C3. `midimsg:batch` output is rejected by `midibin`'s batch encoder  — **High** (R9, R1)
**Location:** `src/midimsg.erl:104-106` vs. `src/midibin.erl:419-420,442-451`
**What's wrong:** `midimsg:batch/2` builds `{midi, {batch, [{id, UUID}, {messages, [Payload, …]}]}}` — a metadata proplist holding *unwrapped* payloads. `midibin:encode({midi, {batch, Batch}})` runs `encode/1` over each list element expecting `{midi, …}` tuples, so it hits `encode({id, UUID})` → `ERR_NON_MIDI` and short-circuits to `[{error, "non-MIDI message"}]`.
**Why it's wrong:** R9 assumes the two align: *"`midibin` encodes a batch as a list of per-message binaries … a hypothetical `midiio:send_batch(Ref, [Bin])` maps one-to-one onto what `midibin` already produces."* That mapping holds for the *test* shape (`{midi, {batch, [{midi,…}, …]}}`, `midibin_tests.erl:233-243`) but **not** for what `midimsg:batch` actually emits — so the one helper a consumer would naturally use to build a batch produces something the codec cannot encode. Cross-ref erlang report #11.
**Fix:** unify the batch contract (see erlang #11). Whichever shape wins, `midi`'s documented reliance on stripping the trailing `{error,_}` element (`encode_batch/2`, `:447-448`) must be preserved and tested against real `midimsg:batch` output.

### C5. Decode completeness — what silently becomes `{unknown, Bin}`  — **Medium** (R1)
**Location:** `src/midibin.erl:158-159` (catch-all), and the gaps it papers over.
**What's wrong:** Because `midi` calls `decode/1` on *every* inbound byte-message, anything without a matching clause reaches subscribers as `{unknown, Bin}` rather than a structured term. Confirmed gaps a live device can emit: multi-byte SysEx (C1); System Common `0xF4`/`0xF5` (undefined — reasonably unknown); **channel-mode messages with non-`0`/`127` value bytes** (e.g. Local Control or All-Notes-Off carrying an unexpected value byte fall through the specific clauses at `:46-81` to the generic CC clause and decode as `{cc, …}` — a *silent reinterpretation*, arguably worse than `{unknown,_}`); and `mono_mode_on` (`0xBn 126`), which returns `ERR_NOT_IMPL` (`:76-77`) — an `{error,_}` mid-stream. `midimsg`'s deprecated `bank_select_msb/lsb` and `channel/1` terms also have no `midibin` encoder.
**Why it's wrong:** `{unknown, Bin}` is the codec's only signal that it failed to parse, but the boundaries are fuzzy: some unsupported inputs return `{unknown,_}`, one returns `{error, not_implemented}`, and some get *reinterpreted* as a different message. `midi` cannot write a single inbound-error policy. (`NEEDS-from-midiio.md` R1 leans on decode covering every device message.)
**Fix:** enumerate and document exactly which inputs yield `{unknown,_}` vs `{error,_}`; decide whether channel-mode-with-odd-value should be `{unknown,_}` rather than silently `cc`; give `mono_mode_on` the same `{unknown,_}`/`{error,_}` treatment as other unsupported messages so the stream contract is uniform.

### C6. `rt_tick/0` hands the consumer an un-encodable, non-existent message  — **Medium**
**Location:** `src/midimsg.erl:296-298`
**What's wrong / why:** A consumer that calls `midimsg:rt_tick()` and sends it gets `ERR_MIDI_UNSUP` from `encode/1` — and the message has no wire meaning anyway (`0xF9`/`0xFD` are spec-Undefined). It is a public function that cannot succeed. Cross-ref erlang report #9, spec report S6.
**Fix:** remove it, or alias it to Timing Clock and document.

## API design & invariants

### C4. Error contract is untagged on one path, forked on another — `midi` has nothing uniform to branch on  — **High** (R4)
**Location:** `src/midifile.erl:37-38` (`{Path, Error}`); `src/midibin.erl:158-159` (`{unknown, Bin}`) vs. `:428-431` (`{error, Reason}`); `include/errors.hrl:1-3` (string reasons).
**What's wrong:** `midifile:read/1` returns `{seq, …}` or an untagged `{Path, {error, Reason}}`. `midibin:decode/1` signals failure as `{unknown, Bin}` (not an `{error,_}`); `midibin:encode/1` signals it as `{error, "string"}`. Three different conventions across the two functions `midi` calls most.
**Why it's wrong:** R4 asks midilib's neighbours to *"return a tagged `{error, _}` for the predictable cases … let a genuinely malformed binary crash."* midilib gives `midi` an untagged file result, a non-`{error,_}` decode failure, and string-reason encode errors — so `midi` must special-case each instead of a single `{ok,_}|{error,_}` discipline. Cross-ref erlang report #14, #15, #16.
**Fix:** `read/1` → `{ok, Seq} | {error, Reason}`; decide whether `decode/1` failure is `{error, {unknown, Bin}}` or a documented distinct channel; convert string reasons to atoms (erlang #16). The new (currently empty) `src/midierrs.erl` is the natural home for a shared error vocabulary + `format_error/1`.

### C7. Channel base differs between the two codecs `midi` will interleave  — **Medium** (R6)
**Location:** `src/midibin.erl:16,169` (1-based) vs. `src/midifile.erl:98,111,123` (0-based).
**What's wrong / why:** `midi` is promised it can *"stream a `.mid` through the exact send path the live API uses."* But `midibin`-decoded live messages present channels 1–16 while `midifile` events expose channels 0–15. Interleaving the two paths yields an off-by-one channel on every file event, with no documented invariant to warn the integrator. Cross-ref erlang report #13.
**Fix:** normalize to one base (1-based, matching `midibin`) in `midifile`, or document the divergence in both module headers and in `midi`'s integration notes.

### C8. `midifile` normalizes note-on-vel-0 → note-off, but normalization is `midi`'s job  — **Medium** (R6)
**Location:** `src/midifile.erl:100-105`
**What's wrong:** `midifile:read/1` folds Note-On velocity 0 into an `off` event (and invents release velocity 64). The family contract is explicit that midilib stays faithful and **`midi`** owns normalization: `NEEDS-from-midiio.md` R6 — *"`midibin:decode/1` does not fold note-on velocity 0 to note-off … midiio must not add its own normalization … the canonical rule `midi` will adopt is vel-0 note-on ⇒ note_off."* `SESSION-BOOTSTRAP-api-planning.md` §6 places normalization in `midi`.
**Why it's wrong:** `midibin` correctly does *not* normalize (clean check #3 below), but `midifile` *does* — so the same logical event arrives normalized from the file path and un-normalized from the live path, defeating `midi`'s "single source of truth" for the quirk. It is also lossy (erlang report #7).
**Fix:** `midifile:read/1` should decode Note-On vel 0 faithfully as a note-on with velocity 0 (preserving the bytes), leaving the fold to `midi`.

## Things I looked for and did not find (clean checks)

1. **`midibin:decode/1` is one-complete-message-per-call** (`midibin.erl:15-159`) — no running-status expansion, no stream reassembly, no multi-message splitting. This is exactly the load-bearing assumption of R1 (*"Every `decode/1` clause matches exactly one whole MIDI message"*), and midilib honours it.
2. **`midibin:decode/1` does not normalize vel-0 note-on** — it decodes `0x9n note 0` as `{note_on, …, velocity 0}` (`:20-23`), satisfying R6's requirement that the codec stay byte-faithful and leave the fold to `midi`. (The `midifile` path violates this — C8 — but the live codec path is correct.)
3. **`encode_batch/2` returns a list of per-message binaries, not a concatenated blob** (`midibin.erl:442-451`), matching R9 / R1 (*"deliver a list of complete-message binaries … not a single concatenated binary"*) and mapping one-to-one onto a future `midiio:send_batch(Ref, [Bin])`.
4. **`encode_batch/2` short-circuits and appends a trailing `{error,_}` element** (`midibin.erl:446-449`) — precisely the behaviour R9 says `midi` will rely on (*"midi strips the trailing error `encode_batch/2` can append … before calling"*). Confirmed present and intentional.
5. **Bytes-at-the-boundary is respected:** midilib produces/consumes `{midi, …}` terms one layer up and raw binaries at the `midiio` edge; it does not try to emit a second structured vocabulary into the transport. This matches the settled boundary (`RESPONSE-to-midi-needs.md`: *"bytes at the boundary, not structured fields"*; `NEEDS-from-midiio.md`: *"No Erlang message-term vocabulary … that's midilib's job"*).
6. **`midibin`'s 1-based channel mapping is round-trip-stable** (`midibin_tests.erl:39-49` exercises channels 8 and 11), so the live path's channel contract with `midi` is at least internally consistent (the only channel issue is the cross-module base mismatch, C7).
7. **The conductor/track split in `midifile:read/1`'s `{seq, Header, ConductorTrack, Tracks}`** (`midifile.erl:36`) matches the shape the consumer expects to feed playback (`SESSION-BOOTSTRAP-api-planning.md` §4: *"the `{seq, Header, ConductorTrack, Tracks}` shape feeds any playback design"*) — the structural contract is right even though the event-level contracts inside it (C2, C7, C8) are not.
